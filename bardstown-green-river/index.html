What am I looking at? (plain-English glossary)
Order
One line of the report = one account buying one product on one date. Counting orders for an account × product is literally how many times they bought it.
Repeat buyer
An account that has bought this brand on 2 or more separate dates in the window — a real, ongoing customer rather than a one-time trial.
Repeat rate
Repeat buyers ÷ total buyers. The share of accounts that have come back for more.
Velocity
Units per month, averaged over the full report window. Higher = moving off the shelf faster at that account.
CORE
The brand's flagship SKU set. An account "carries the CORE" only when it has bought every CORE SKU at least once. Green River CORE = Bourbon, Full Proof, Rye, Wheated, Honey (5). Bardstown Bourbon CORE = Bottled-in-Bond, Bourbon, Double Barrel Rye, High Wheat (4).
Near-CORE
An account that's carrying every CORE SKU except exactly one — the single easiest upsell to complete the set.
Distribution Area
The rep's territory / route, as assigned in the sales system.
