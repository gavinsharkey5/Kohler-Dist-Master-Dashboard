Every on-premise account evaluated against Sun Cruiser and White Claw, sized by opportunity and matched with the flavor gap Carbliss can fill. All case counts below are Sun Cruiser + White Claw only. Tap an account for the pitch.

Without Carbliss only

How to read this: every case count on this page is Sun Cruiser + White Claw only. The High-Velocity tab is accounts with active 2026 volume, sorted biggest opportunity first. The Without Sun Cruiser/White Claw tab is accounts with zero cases so far in 2026 — sorted by what they used to buy, biggest first, so the best re-engagement targets surface. The Without Carbliss only toggle hides any account that already carries a Carbliss flavor.

Each pitch is a handful of bullets, not a script: best-selling item and whether it's new or established, how many SKUs move through the account, and a Carbliss flavor recommendation. That flavor is deliberately not whatever's already selling well through Sun Cruiser or White Claw — that craving's already covered — it's a genuine gap on their current menu, so Carbliss adds breadth instead of competing head-on, plus a price comparison against what's already on shelf.

New = zero volume last year, all of it new in 2026. Has Carbliss = already carries at least one Carbliss flavor; the pitch focuses on the next flavor to add.

Source: RDE Carbliss Eval vs Sun Cruiser & White Claw exports (account-level cases/buyers, and SKU-level price & volume). Update by re-exporting both reports into accounts.csv / price_vol.csv and running generate.py in this folder.
