Each pitch is a handful of bullets, not a script: best-selling item and whether it's new or established, how many SKUs move through the account, and a Carbliss flavor recommendation. That flavor is deliberately not whatever's already selling well through Sun Cruiser or White Claw — that craving's already covered — it's a genuine gap on their current menu, so Carbliss adds breadth instead of competing head-on.

New = zero volume last year, all of it new in 2026. Has Carbliss = already carries at least one Carbliss flavor; the pitch focuses on the next flavor to add.
