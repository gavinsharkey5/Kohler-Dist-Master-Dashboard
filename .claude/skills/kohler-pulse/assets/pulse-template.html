🍺 Draft Pulse

We hold just over half of Core Market draft. 51% of the 5,255 handles surveyed across 624 core accounts are ours. Passaic and Morris 3 run strongest at better than 56%, while Morris 1 sits closer to 39%. No single competitor comes near us — Stella Artois and Guinness are the largest outside brands at roughly 5% of core handles each.

Blue Moon and Yuengling own the most handles. Miller Lite gets the most out of each one. Blue Moon and Yuengling each carry roughly 300 core lines, but Miller Lite moves noticeably more beer per handle than any of our other large-footprint brands. Our biggest tap brand is not our most productive one.

A few small brands are turning faster than anything else we pour. Hofbrau, Weihenstephan and Garage Beer sit on single-digit handle counts yet out-turn our major brands per line — and all three are growing on keg volume this year.

Our share of the handles is steady; the mix pouring through them is not. Draft volume is off about 3% on the year. Peroni is up nearly 20%, Cape May a third, and Garage Beer has more than tripled, while Coors Light, Sam Adams and Pabst are all giving back kegs.

🟢 Winning

Off-premise MPOs are closing in on the finish line. Powierski, Melo, Barreca, Payton, Ehlers and Lagala are all near completion on the August card, with Corona Premier already behind most of that group.

Our strongest display conversion is coming from the smaller books. Phil Ernst and Mike Ast are averaging better than a display per off-premise account across their entire base — the best rate in the company.

The growth brands are carrying real momentum into the fall. Sun Cruiser, Garage Beer, Pacifico, Modelo Chelada and Sapporo are all up double digits or better on the year.

🍷 Wine & Spirits

The quickest volume available is in distribution we have already won. A sizeable group of accounts that previously carried Wine & Spirits products have not reordered in the last sixty days. Because the placement is already established, reactivating those accounts may be a faster path to cases than starting from zero.

We are adding new buyers, but losing them faster. Roughly two accounts have gone quiet this year for every new buyer we have brought in. The portfolio is finding new homes — holding onto them is where the direction is being decided.

The Southern District is converting its available opportunity well. Measured against the accounts and brands those reps can actually sell, Southern is activating a stronger share of its book than Core Market is. Their small-format accounts appear to suit this portfolio.

🟡 Open Opportunities

Roughly one in five core accounts we survey carries no Kohler handle at all. 139 of the 624 accounts we walked pour none of our draft. Some will be contract houses, but that is a long list in rooms we already call on.

Corona Premier has shown the placement is there — and there is still room to expand it. Several reps have already completed the objective. Additional target accounts remain available across the market, making this one of the cleaner places to add distribution before the month closes.

Angry Orchard has the lines but not the pull. It carries a respectable handle count for a cider, yet turns below our portfolio average per line, and a large share of its target list is still open.

👀 Watching

Molson Coors performance needs to be viewed alongside inventory availability. Recent out-of-stocks and backorders have affected several of the same items tied to current programs. Before drawing conclusions about the field, it is worth understanding what was actually available to sell.

Southern District tap data is still developing. The survey base there is much smaller than Core Market’s and weighted toward the largest craft rooms, so the current share numbers should be read as directional.

The display pace has eased after a strong July finish. August is running below the late-July pace, but the display competition is still close enough that the final selling days could change the standings.

🎯 Where to Lean In

The fast-turning small brands may deserve more handles. Hofbrau, Weihenstephan and Garage Beer are out-turning our major brands on a handful of lines apiece.

Corona Premier still has accessible placements available. The reps who finished the program have already shown the objective is achievable.

Wine & Spirits reorders may be the quickest incremental volume we have. Previously won placements let us generate cases without starting from zero.

The line for the sales meeting: we hold half the draft handles in our core market and the shelf side is finishing the month well — the open business is in what pours through the lines we already own, and in Wine & Spirits accounts that already know the product.
