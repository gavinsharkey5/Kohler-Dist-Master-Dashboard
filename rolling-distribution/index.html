How things are counted. One row in the source is one product at one account for one month. A buyer is an account whose net cases in the period are above zero, counted once per row of the table (an account buying three Blue Moon packages is 1 Blue Moon buyer, 3 placements). A placement is one product at one account with net cases above zero in the period. Cases are net of returns and credits. "Any invoice activity" switches buyers and placements to Fusion's own flag, which also counts accounts whose period nets to zero or negative.
Periods are whole calendar months. Rep and district manager are the current assignment applied to all history. "Prior period" is the previous non-overlapping window of the same length; "prior year" is the same months one year earlier. Source: Fusion product × account exports merged by rolling-distribution/generate.py.
