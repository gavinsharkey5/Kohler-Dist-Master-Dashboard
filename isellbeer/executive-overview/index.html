    Data & methodology notes
    • "Handles" = the "# of Taps" field summed across every surveyed row, not row count — an account with 3 handles of one brand counts as 3, not 1.
    • Us/Them status is the tap-audit engine's "Corrected Distributor" ruling (product catalog + territory rules), the same authoritative source used by the rep-facing tap tracker.
    • Three area-label data issues were corrected here: "Passaic-FF" is folded into "Passaic"; "Sales" (a placeholder for unassigned rows) is reassigned from each account's City, falling back to Address for the handful of cities appearing nowhere else in the export; "Morris 2" is real but isn't one of the named core or non-focus areas, so it appears in "Other Areas" rather than being folded into Morris 1/3.
    • Brand names differ between the tap-survey system and Encompass (e.g. "Sam Adams Seasonal" vs. "Samuel Adams", "Miller Lite" vs. "Lite") — velocity figures are reconciled via the audit workbook's own Brand Crosswalk sheet plus a supplier-name fallback, and should be read as directional, not exact to the unit.
    • Velocity is computed only at the accounts where both a tap survey row and an Encompass sales record exist for the same customer number (see the note above the velocity table for the match rate) — it is not a company-wide sales total.
    • Account Control counts only core-market accounts with at least one classified handle — accounts surveyed with zero recorded handles are excluded from the bands (which is why its account count is lower than the hero's "accounts surveyed").
    • Segment Battleground uses the audit workbook's own segment sheets (Encompass Sub-Segment first, then iSellBeer's survey tag) wherever those map cleanly to a tier; brands whose workbook rows carry only style labels ("Wheat Beer", "Pilsner And Pale Lager") fall back to this page's brand-name heuristic. "Their #1" / "Our #1" exclude generic catch-all labels like "Other Supplier", but the bar totals include every handle.
    • "Brands Pouring on Both Sides" lists brand families with at least 2 handles on each side in the core market — the same brand sold through us at one account and through a competing distributor at another.
    • Velocity quadrants split brands (5+ matched handles) at the median handle count and median units-per-handle — "median" meaning half the brands sit above and half below, so the quadrant labels are relative to our own portfolio, not an absolute standard.
