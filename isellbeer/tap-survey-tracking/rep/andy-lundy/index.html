How to Read This Page

How to read this: each rep card shows a pie of their book's tap handles — Ours vs. Competitor (by # of Taps, not just row count, so an account with 3 handles of the same brand counts as 3). Expand a rep to see their accounts — sorted by tap count by default, or flip that rep’s list to most-recent-visit-first with the Sort buttons; expand an account to see its individual brands, each tagged Ours or Competitor.

By Brand tab: the same tap handles, regrouped by brand family instead of rep — ranked by total taps surveyed. Expand a brand to see its distribution by County/Area, Rep, and City, each with the same Ours-vs-Competitor mix. Use the search box to jump straight to a brand instead of scrolling; any other filter set above (rep, county, status) narrows this view too, so you can e.g. see one rep's distribution of a single brand.

60-day resurvey warning: reps are expected to resurvey every account within 60 days. An account's "Visited" date turns into Due in Nd starting 7 days out, and Overdue Nd once past 60 — both shown on the account row, rolled up as a count on each rep's card, and totaled in the "60-Day Resurvey" tile above. This is computed live against today's date in your browser, not baked into the data export, so it stays accurate between refreshes even if the underlying survey data doesn't change.

US / THEM source: built from the tap-audit engine's own working file (the iSellBeer Corrected/US vs THEM Mediator workbook), not a flat pre-corrected export. Every brand is checked against Kohler's product catalog and Brand Family Territory rules; where iSellBeer's own survey flag disagrees with that check, the row is marked ⇆ corrected and hovering the badge shows why. were corrected this way.

Photos: shown per account using the real photo link behind each survey's "Photos" cell — a plain CSV export of this same report flattens that link to text, so a future refresh from a .csv (instead of the full mediator workbook) would lose photos again.

What’s on tap now, not everything ever surveyed: an account can be surveyed more than once, and every number here — each account’s handles, the rep and brand rollups, the tiles and target lists — counts only its most recent survey pass. Earlier passes are never added on top, so a brand poured at both visits counts once, and a brand since taken off the wall doesn’t count at all.

Survey history: an account surveyed more than once carries a ↻ N passes badge on its row. Expand the account and open Survey history to page through every pass on record, see each one’s own lineup and handle count, and read what changed — brands added, removed, and retained — between the previous pass and today’s. Nothing in that section feeds any total on this page.
