Every surveyed tap, grouped by rep → account → brand. Each rep's book of accounts is scored Ours vs. Competitor by tap handle; drill into an account to see its brands, when it was last visited, and its most recent photo.

Ours — carried & in territory Competitor Unverified — no US/THEM flag on file

How to read this: each rep card shows a pie of their book's tap handles — Ours vs. Competitor (by # of Taps, not just row count, so an account with 3 handles of the same brand counts as 3). Expand a rep to see their accounts, sorted by tap count; expand an account to see its individual brands, each tagged Ours or Competitor.

US / THEM source: the Distributor column in the export is used as-is here — it's expected to already be corrected against your Encompass territory rules before this file is produced (see the tap-audit process). This build does not re-derive it from the product catalog / territory tables itself.

Photos: shown per account when the source export carries a real photo link. A plain CSV export flattens that link to text, so if this was built from a .csv, no photos will show until it's rebuilt from an .xlsx export of the same report.

Visit date: this export is one snapshot in time — each account appears with a single visit date. Refresh by re-exporting and re-running generate.py; a future export with repeat visits per account would show the most recent one.
