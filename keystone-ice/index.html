Accounts sold by day

Goals are whole accounts. Kohler's workbook sets each rep's qualifier at 40% of their 2026 off-premise buyer base and the bonus at 50%, which can land on a fraction. Those are rounded up, so the number on your card is exactly how many accounts you need. Rounding up changes nothing about who qualifies — you can't sell part of an account.

Accounts, not orders. An account that reorders still counts once. That's why the daily bars don't add up to the house total — the same account can appear on more than one day.

Cases don't count here. The qualifier, the bonus and both top-performer awards are all scored on how many accounts you sell, not how much volume goes into them.
