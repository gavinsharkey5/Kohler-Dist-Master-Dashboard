How each number is calculated
Locations audited
Count of saved locations (each cooler, bar or stand you logged is one location).
Brand share
Brand's cooler facings + tap handles ÷ all facings + tap handles audited.
Facings share
Brand's cooler facings ÷ all cooler facings.
Draft share
Brand's tap handles ÷ all tap handles.
Cooler share
% of cooler locations where the brand has at least one facing.
Distribution
% of all audited locations (cooler or tap) carrying the brand.
Share by section / level
Brand share recomputed inside that section or level only.
Our brands
Every brand added from an "Ours" picker (or written in as ours), combined.
