Data sources & methodology — exactly how every column is calculated

Source files

  1. Boston Beer forecast export (from the Boston Beer online ordering portal) — Product, Distributor, BBC SKU, Customer SKU, then a pair of columns per week: "<date> F" is Boston Beer's own system-generated forecast, "<date> A" is your manager's confirmed order for that week (blank until entered). Feeds: Product/SKU, Boston Beer Forecast, On File.
  2. Encompass "Boston Beer Inventory Report" — Supplier Product ID, Available, L4 Average, L8 Average, the last 5 weeks of case sales, Last Receive Date/Quantity. Feeds: Available, 4-wk Avg, Recent Trend, and (with the Target/Tolerance sliders) Weeks of Supply and Status.
  3. Encompass "Packages" reference export — maps each package description (e.g. "1/6 BBL Keg") to a Case Equiv conversion factor. Feeds: the whole-unit rounding on Recommended (e.g. why a keg SKU recommends "3", not "6.94").
  4. Encompass "ForecastReport" — per SKU, the actual Boston Beer order lead time in days plus 52 weeks of trailing sell-through history. Feeds: the lead-time-based correction window on Recommended, and the same-week-last-year fallback for SKUs with no current sales.
  5. Encompass "Products" export — maps each Supplier Product Num to its clean Encompass product name. Feeds: the Product column display name.

The Recommended formula

Every SKU uses exactly one of three methods, shown per-row as "Recommended via":

1. Velocity-based (SKUs with 4-wk Avg > 0 and a matched Available figure — most SKUs):

  1. Target inventory position = Target weeks of supply × 4-wk Avg.
  2. Gap = Target inventory position − Available.
  3. Correction weeks = Lead time in days (from ForecastReport) ÷ 7, rounded, minimum 1 (21 days → 3 weeks for this data set).
  4. Per-week correction = Gap ÷ Correction weeks.
  5. Shape = that week's Boston Beer Forecast ÷ the average Boston Beer Forecast across all open weeks — this preserves known seasonal ramps (e.g. Octoberfest) instead of flattening every week to the same number.
  6. Each week's raw recommendation = (4-wk Avg × Shape) + Per-week correction (correction only applied to the first Correction-weeks weeks), floored at 0.
  7. Rounded to the nearest whole physically-orderable unit using the SKU's Case Equiv from the Packages export (e.g. nearest whole keg), then converted back to case-equivalents for display/export.

2. Seasonal fallback (no current velocity, but ForecastReport history exists): uses the actual volume from the same calendar week one year earlier (±5 days), case-pack rounded the same way.

3. Passthrough (no velocity and no usable history): Boston Beer's own forecast, unchanged.
