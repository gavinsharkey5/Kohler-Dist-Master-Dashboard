Trend Forecast vs. Boston Beer Forecast

How are the trend forecast, the 13-wk trend %, and the tint calculated?

13-wk trend % = (this year's trailing-13-week case total ÷ last year's trailing-13-week case total) − 1, using the exact 13-week windows L13_Trend.csv reports (shown just above this table). For Sam Adams Cherry Wheat 4/6/12oz Btl (SA2712): this year 699 cases ÷ last year 804 cases − 1 = −13%.

Trend forecast (the bold number in each week) = last year's actual cases for that same retail week × (1 + 13-wk trend %). Continuing SA2712 (−13% trend): last year's week-of-7/26 actual was 25 cases, so 25 × (1 − 13%) = 22 — that week's trend forecast. Hover any trend forecast box to see this exact math for that product and week.

If a SKU has no L13_Trend.csv row, or its last-year trailing-13-week total is zero or negative (e.g. a mostly-returns window, nothing positive to measure growth against), there's no % to apply — that SKU's boxes get a dashed border and simply carry last year's actual cases forward as-is, with no growth/decline adjustment.

BBC diff % (the small text under each trend forecast) is how far Boston Beer's own forecast is from the trend forecast: (BBC forecast − Trend forecast) / Trend forecast. For example, Angry Orchard Crisp (OA7863), week of 7/25: the trend forecast is 161 cases, Boston Beer forecasts 98, so (98 − 161) / 161 = −39% — Boston Beer's forecast is 39% lower than what the recent trend suggests. Any week off by more than 10% either direction gets tinted amber (BBC above trend) or teal (BBC below trend).
