Trend Forecast vs. Boston Beer Forecast

What does the % next to BBC mean?

It's how far Boston Beer's forecast is from the trend forecast: (BBC forecast − Trend forecast) / Trend forecast. For example, Angry Orchard Crisp (OA7863), week of 7/25: the trend forecast is 161 cases (last year's actual for that week × its +12% 13-week trend), Boston Beer forecasts 98, so (98 − 161) / 161 = −39% — Boston Beer's forecast is 39% lower than what the recent trend suggests. Any week off by more than 10% either direction gets tinted. Both numbers below are editable if you want to test a different scenario — the % recalculates as you type.
