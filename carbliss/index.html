A note on categories: White Claw is a hard seltzer, not an RTD cocktail. Sun Cruiser and Carbliss both are. They're grouped together in this tracker as "RTD/seltzer volume" because they compete for the same shelf, the same cooler space, and the same low-ABV, portable, not-beer occasion — not because they're the same product. Treat White Claw volume as a proxy for "this account already has that kind of customer," not literal RTD demand.

Gap account = active Sun Cruiser and/or White Claw volume at or above the threshold you set, with zero Carbliss cases recorded. Adjust the on-prem / off-prem thresholds above — there's no single right cutoff, so this is left as a dial, not baked in.

On-Premise goal is fixed at 331 accounts carrying Carbliss (menu/tap placements). Off-Premise has no ceiling — it's tracked as a running count with momentum vs. the last snapshot, not a countdown.

Figures reflect the Fusion export loaded at build time. Pitches are templated from each account's own volume data — same phrasing logic every time, so reps can trust what's on the card without re-checking it.

Pitch detail: each pitch pulls the account's single top-selling Sun Cruiser/White Claw SKU, maps it to the closest Carbliss flavor by name (exact matches only — ambiguous ones default to Black Cherry, Carbliss's top seller, and say so), and compares it to Carbliss's average case price for that channel ($54.43/case on-prem, $51.50/case off-prem). Carbliss runs structurally higher than both competitors on a per-case basis (avg $52 vs. $40 Sun Cruiser vs. $31 White Claw) — most pitches will read as a premium ask, which is accurate, not a bug. Price is compared $/case as reported and isn't adjusted for pack-size differences between brands.

Trend: pitches now cite year-over-year case movement on the account's top SKU (Jan–Jul 2025 vs. the same window in 2026) when the swing is 15%+ in either direction, or call out a SKU that's brand new to the account this year. Flat accounts (under 15% either way) skip the trend line rather than force one.

City comes from the original Fusion export, not parsed from the account name — it's accurate for all 918 accounts, so no name-guessing was needed. No market-character claims (nightlife, college-town, etc.) are made about any city; that data isn't verified here and guessing at it risks stereotyping, so it's deliberately left out.

Win-Back Accounts = accounts with real Sun Cruiser/White Claw case volume in 2025 that show zero combined volume in 2026. They dropped the category completely, not just Carbliss — a different conversation than the gap list above, and the pitches reflect that: they reference 2025 volume (since 2026 is zero) and suggest asking what changed before re-pitching, rather than assuming a straightforward add.

Win-back price note: since these accounts aren't buying anything right now, the pitch cites what they used to pay for their top 2025 SKU next to Carbliss's current channel price — framed as useful context to walk in with, not a swap decision like the gap pitches.
