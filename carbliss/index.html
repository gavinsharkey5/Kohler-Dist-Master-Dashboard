A note on categories: White Claw is a hard seltzer, not an RTD cocktail. Sun Cruiser and Carbliss both are. They're grouped together in this tracker as "RTD/seltzer volume" because they compete for the same shelf, the same cooler space, and the same low-ABV, portable, not-beer occasion — not because they're the same product. Treat White Claw volume as a proxy for "this account already has that kind of customer," not literal RTD demand.

Goal is 331 on-premise accounts carrying Carbliss (menu/tap placements). This tracker is scoped to on-premise only — off-premise is a separate push, not tracked here for now.

Figures reflect the Fusion export loaded at build time. Pitches are templated from each account's own volume data — same phrasing logic every time, so reps can trust what's on the card without re-checking it.

Pitch detail: each pitch pulls the account's single top-selling Sun Cruiser/White Claw SKU, maps it to the closest Carbliss flavor by name (exact matches only — ambiguous ones default to Black Cherry, Carbliss's top seller, and say so), and compares it to Carbliss's average on-premise case price (~$54.39/case). Carbliss runs structurally higher than both competitors on a per-case basis — most pitches will read as a premium ask, which is accurate, not a bug. Price is compared $/case as reported and isn't adjusted for pack-size differences between brands.

Trend: pitches now cite year-over-year case movement on the account's top SKU (Jan–Jul 2025 vs. the same window in 2026) when the swing is 15%+ in either direction, or call out a SKU that's brand new to the account this year. Flat accounts (under 15% either way) skip the trend line rather than force one.

Flavor gap: a second, separate pitch angle available on 217 of 465 accounts — the ones with data in the full FMB/RTD/seltzer competitive file (not just Sun Cruiser/White Claw). This looks across every FMB/RTD/seltzer product the account currently carries (2026 volume, single-flavor SKUs only — Variety/Sampler/Mix/Original/Half & Half packs are excluded since they don't name one specific flavor) and flags a Carbliss flavor that's missing from the account's entire lineup, not just from Sun Cruiser/White Claw. This is a "fills a real hole" angle, distinct from the SKU-match line above it, which is a "closest match to what's already selling" angle. When an account has no data in that file, or is already covered on every Carbliss flavor, this line is skipped rather than guessed.

City is backfilled from the prior full Fusion export where available (accurate for 464 of 465 tracked accounts); it's not parsed or guessed from the account name. No market-character claims (nightlife, college-town, etc.) are made about any city; that data isn't verified here and guessing at it risks stereotyping, so it's deliberately left out.

Win-Back Accounts = accounts with real Sun Cruiser/White Claw case volume in 2025 that show zero combined volume in 2026.
