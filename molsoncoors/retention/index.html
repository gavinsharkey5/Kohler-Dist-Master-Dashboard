What am I looking at? (plain-English glossary)
Placement
One account buying one Molson Coors product during the Spring Program (Jan 1–Apr 30, 2026). An account that bought 3 different products counts as 3 placements.
Retained
A placement counts as retained once that same account buys that same product again after the program, at or above the case floor selected below.
Retention rate
Retained placements ÷ total placements. The share of what got placed that's actually sticking.
Case floor
The "Retained = re-buy of at least…" control below. Sets the minimum cases an account must re-buy for it to count as a real re-buy, not just a token top-off.
Re-buy
Any purchase of a placed product that happens after the program ended (May 1 onward).
Regular re-buyer
A retained placement that's been re-ordered on 4 or more separate dates — a habitual, sticky customer for that product.
Velocity
How fast a retained product is selling, in cases per week. Higher = moving off the shelf/tap faster.
Distro goal
The rep's original Spring Distribution Program placement goal, from the goals file — shown for context next to what's actually sticking.
Partial window
We're only partway through the 90-day retention window, so these numbers will keep climbing as more time passes. Today's figures are a floor, not the final result.
