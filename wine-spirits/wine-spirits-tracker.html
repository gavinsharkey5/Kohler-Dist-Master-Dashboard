Reading the YoY numbers: FY2025 figures reflect the full calendar year. FY2026 figures are year-to-date through the most recent data pull — not a full year. Any YoY % comparison here is therefore full-year vs. partial-year and will read artificially negative purely from the calendar, not necessarily from execution. Use the New / Active / Lapsed buyer flags and % Activated for the cleanest read on rep execution; treat the dollar YoY column as directional until we're comparing full year to full year (or a same-period YTD once we have a 2025 YTD cut).

Rep Performance Summary

What do these columns mean?
Eligible Accts
Accounts on this rep's book that are known to carry wine & spirits (they had W&S activity in FY25 or FY26). This is the rep's realistic, addressable universe — not every account they're assigned, just the ones that can actually buy W&S.
Activated FY26
Of those eligible accounts, how many have bought at least one W&S item so far in FY26.
% Activated
Activated FY26 ÷ Eligible Accts. The share of the rep's addressable book that's currently buying W&S.
New Buyers
Accounts that bought W&S in FY26 but did NOT buy any in FY25 — brand new to the category this year.
Lapsed
Accounts that bought W&S in FY25 but have not bought anything yet in FY26 — at risk of being lost.
FY25 $Vol
Total wine & spirits dollar sales for the full 2025 calendar year.
FY26 $Vol (YTD)
Total wine & spirits dollar sales so far in 2026. This is year-to-date, not a full year, so it will naturally be smaller than FY25.
YoY %
% change from FY25 $Vol to FY26 $Vol. Because FY25 is a full year and FY26 is only YTD, this will read negative even for a rep who's pacing fine — use it directionally, not as a final verdict.

Account-Level Detail

What do these columns mean?
Channel
On Premise = bars, restaurants, hotels (drink on site). Off Premise = liquor/retail stores (take home).
Status: Active
Bought W&S in both FY25 and FY26.
Status: New
Bought W&S in FY26 but had no FY25 purchases — a brand-new W&S buyer this year.
Status: Lapsed
Bought W&S in FY25 but hasn't bought anything yet in FY26 — a buyer we're losing.
Status: Non-Buyer
On the eligible list but shows no W&S purchases in either year — an untapped account.
FY25 $Vol / FY26 $Vol (YTD)
Total W&S dollar sales at this account for the full FY2025 year vs. FY2026 so far (year-to-date).
YoY %
% change from FY25 to FY26 $Vol. Since FY26 is YTD only, this reads negative by default even for accounts pacing normally.
Brands (FY26)
Which brand families this account has actually purchased so far this year — use this to spot placement gaps.
