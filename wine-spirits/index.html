How to read this — cases only, and everything follows the date range above
Pick any range of months and the whole dashboard recalculates for it, next to a comparable prior period. Volume is measured in cases everywhere: the Encompass exports measure volume in a selling size that differs by item (a bottle for some items, a full case for others), and every figure is converted to cases at the product level before anything is added up. The account file is monthly, so ranges snap to whole months. Distribution is measured as buying accounts in the range against the same number of months a year earlier — never a partial period against a full one. Margins are realized figures (case price minus laid-in cost, weighted by cases actually sold) from real invoice transactions; rows carrying no price at all are load-sheet movements rather than sales and are excluded.

What do these columns mean?
Buyers
Distinct accounts that bought at least one case inside the selected range.
Comparison
The same count over the comparison period set above — same number of months, never a partial vs a full period.
Gained / Lost
Accounts buying in the range that weren't buying in the comparison period, and vice versa. Tap a row to see them by name.
90-Day Buyers
Distinct accounts buying in the last 3 months of the selected range — the closest the monthly source data gets to a rolling 90 days.
Cases
Case volume in the range. Nothing here is counted in bottles or eaches.
