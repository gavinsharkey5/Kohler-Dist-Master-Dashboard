Brand Trend vs. Goal

Where each brand's current-year trend stands against its 2026 Brewery Goal % and Kohler Goal %, so brand managers can see who's ahead or behind pace and recalibrate for the back half of the year. "Trend" is each brand's year-over-year Case Equivalent change for the identical date range in both years (a true apples-to-apples comparison). Goal % is editable per brand — adjust either track up or down to see its case target and gap recalculate live. Edits are local to your browser only and don't save back to the workbook; use Export CSV to keep a copy.

'26 Proj. = 2026 YTD CE + (2025 Finish − 2025 YTD CE) × (1 + Trend %) — this year's cases so far, plus the rest of 2025 grown at this year's trend rate.

Gap = Trend % − Goal % — negative (red) is behind that goal, zero or positive (green) is on pace or ahead. Editing a Goal % recalculates its Gap and Goal CE immediately.

Methodology & data notes

Trend %: each brand's Case Equivalent volume for the current comparable-YTD window vs. the same window last year (the exact dates are noted at the top of the page), straight from the Encompass comparison report — not a projection.

Goal %: each brand's 2026 Brewery Goal and Kohler Goal, pulled from the 2026 planning workbook (the same one used to set targets at the start of the year).

Gap: Trend % minus Goal %. Negative (red) means the brand is trending behind that goal; zero or positive (green) means it's on pace or ahead.

Data quality caveats (click to expand)
    No goal, no comparison. These brands had zero recorded sales in the prior-year comparable window and no Brewery or Kohler goal % in the 2026 planning workbook — almost always because they launched after the plan was built (e.g. Carbliss, Monaco, Noca). There's nothing to measure them against yet; once they get a 2026 goal assigned, they'll move into the main By Brand Family tab on the next refresh.

    Methodology & data notes

    Trend %: each supplier's total Case Equivalent volume for the current comparable-YTD window vs. the same window last year, straight from the Encompass comparison report's own supplier subtotal row (not a re-sum of the By Brand Family tab's rows, so it still reconciles even for brands that tab excludes or relabels).

    Goal %: each supplier's 2026 Brewery Goal and Kohler Goal, pulled from that supplier's own header row in the 2026 planning workbook (same workbook, same columns as the brand-level goals).

    Coverage: a supplier only appears here if it has both a goal in the planning workbook AND recorded volume in the YTD comparison in either year — 14 workbook suppliers (e.g. Suntory, Iron Horse, Heavy Seas) had zero volume in both years and so don't appear in the source export at all.
